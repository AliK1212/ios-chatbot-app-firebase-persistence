# SafeHMO Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 18, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to SafeHMO. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile app (available on iOS and Android platforms) and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

## 2. Data We Collect

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you which we have grouped together as follows:

- **Identity Data** includes first name, last name, username or similar identifier.
- **Contact Data** includes email address and telephone numbers.
- **Technical Data** includes device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, internet protocol (IP) address, your login data, and other technology on the mobile devices you use to access this app.
- **Usage Data** includes information about how you use our mobile app and services.
- **Chat Data** includes messages you send to our AI assistant and the responses you receive.
- **Document Data** includes PDF documents and other files you upload to the app for processing.
- **Mobile Device Data** includes device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

- To register you as a new user
- To provide and improve our AI assistant services
- To process and analyze your documents
- To manage our relationship with you
- To administer and protect our business and this app
- To deliver relevant app content and advertisements to you
- To use data analytics to improve our app, products/services, marketing, customer relationships and experiences

## 4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered or disclosed. In addition, we limit access to your personal data to those employees, agents, contractors and other third parties who have a business need to know.

## 5. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

## 6. Your Legal Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to:

- Request access to your personal data
- Request correction of your personal data
- Request erasure of your personal data
- Object to processing of your personal data
- Request restriction of processing your personal data
- Request transfer of your personal data
- Right to withdraw consent

## 7. Third-Party Links

This app may include links to third-party websites, plug-ins and applications. Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements.

## 8. AI Processing and OpenAI Integration

Our app uses OpenAI's API services to process text and generate responses for our PDF chatbot feature. When you use this feature:

- Your document content and chat messages are sent to OpenAI for processing
- OpenAI processes this data in accordance with their privacy policy (available at https://openai.com/privacy)
- We do not grant OpenAI permission to use your data to train their models
- We track token usage for billing and optimization purposes
- We implement security measures to protect data in transit to and from OpenAI

## 9. Mobile App Privacy Information

Our SafeHMO app is exclusively available as a mobile application for iOS and Android devices. As a mobile-only application, we collect and process data specifically related to mobile usage:

### 9.1 Mobile Permissions

Our mobile app may request certain permissions to access your device's functionality, including:

- **Camera**: To scan documents (only accessed when you explicitly use the scan feature)
- **Storage/Files**: To access and upload PDF documents
- **Network Access**: To communicate with our servers and the OpenAI API

You can manage these permissions through your device settings at any time.

### 9.2 Push Notifications

With your consent, we may send push notifications to your mobile device. You can disable push notifications at any time by adjusting your device settings.

### 9.3 App Store Data Collection

Apple App Store and Google Play Store may collect certain usage and crash data in accordance with their respective privacy policies. This collection is governed by Apple and Google and is subject to their terms.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than the country in which you are resident. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country. Specifically, our servers are located in the European Union, and we use OpenAI's services which process data in the United States.

We have implemented appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this privacy policy.

## 11. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. For significant changes, we may also provide additional notice within the app.
